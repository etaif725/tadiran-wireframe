# Section-specific image direction

The three opening-story images are reserved for that story. New contextual photography is composed for the actual layout, rather than reserving the same side for copy. Brand consistency comes from warm daylight, ivory, oak, navy and restrained cyan. No mandatory baked-in light trail.

## Layouts

- Healthcare, assisted living, financial services: intimate centered human moments, image on right and separate text on left.
- Transportation, education, alarm systems: working context, image on left and separate text on right.
- Hospitality and utilities: full-width environmental panoramas with the heading above the image.
- OmniCX, AVA, Mobile Touch: centered product-use photography in a left image panel. Actual supplied UI is separate evidence.
- Aeonix and recording/quality: right image panel with a distinct working moment.
- Analytics: room-wide composition, analyst at left, heading above.
- Enterprise, critical communications and cloud: panoramic environments, heading above.
- Omnichannel CX and security: centered contextual scenes in right image panel.
- AI, integrations and MSO: left image panel; integrations uses practical hands/equipment detail.
- OEM: close-up engineering craft, right image panel.
- Company and industry overview: full-width environmental imagery.
- Partners and solution overview: balanced collaboration/overhead work detail, left image panel.
- Resources: elevated desk detail, right image panel.
- Homepage product features: photo and real product interface in adjacent panels, with alternating proportions; no screenshots covering a person's body.

## Motion

Remove the visible motion-free option. Native scrolling controls camera movement, clip reveals, staggered copy, travelling line and connective transition captions. Reduce motion automatically for OS preference. Mobile receives a compact scroll composition rather than the desktop crop forced into a narrow column.

Exact generation prompts and image provenance are recorded alongside the delivered asset mapping.
